Why tokenized

The assets you know.
Without the old limits.

Tokenized assets are stocks, gold, US Treasuries and property issued as tokens on a blockchain. The same investments, with better rules for you.

  • The old way: open Monday to Friday, 9:30 to 4. Tokenized: open any hour, any day.
    The old way↻Mon–Fri, 9:30 to 4

    Closed nights, weekends and holidays.

    TokenizedAny hour, any day

    Trade at midnight, on Sundays and on holidays.

  • The old way: settles in one to two business days. Tokenized: settles in seconds.
    The old way↻Settles in 1–2 days

    Your money waits in between.

    TokenizedSettles in seconds

    Your money and your asset move at the same moment.

  • The old way: whole shares and minimums. Tokenized: start with a few dollars.
    The old way↻Whole shares, big minimums

    Expensive assets stay out of reach.

    TokenizedStart with a few dollars

    Own 0.05 of a share or half a gram of gold.

  • The old way: you need a broker in the right country. Tokenized: a wallet is often all you need.
    The old way↻A broker in the right country

    Most of the world can't open one.

    TokenizedA wallet is enough

    Reach US stocks and Treasuries from much of the world.

  • The old way: someone else holds it for you. Tokenized: it sits in your own wallet.
    The old way↻Someone else holds it

    You own a line in their books.

    TokenizedIt's in your wallet

    Hold it, move it or sell it whenever you like.

  • The old way: idle cash earns nothing. Tokenized: tokenized Treasuries can pay interest on stablecoins.
    The old way↻Idle cash earns nothing

    Stablecoins just sit there.

    TokenizedCash that grows

    Tokenized Treasuries pay interest, a little every day.
