My pick

Go with Companion.

It sells the thing people are actually signing up for: owning stocks, gold and Treasuries from a wallet, any hour, from a few dollars, with someone in their corner. The day-to-night journey says "24/7" without a single chart, and the bird in the corner makes "never alone" something you feel while scrolling rather than a claim.

The Vault concept from the first round is retired. Its whole idea was looking inside tokens, which is the analysis-first angle you asked to move away from.

In every concept

  • Copy covers stocks and tokenized assets. The live page only covers stocks and says 18 tools; the plan says 19.
  • The waitlist asks two optional questions, what to own or watch and US or not, so you have the geography data §5 needs for the rail decision. The server keeps them and adds them to the CSV export.
  • Each form tags its source (companion-hero, verdict-foot…), so an A/B test shows which page converts.
  • No third-party scripts, so they fit the current CSP. Reduced-motion support, complete without JavaScript, and "Research, not financial advice" on every page.

To ship one

  1. Copy concepts/<name>.html over public/index.html.
  2. Move <name>.js to public/, point the script tag at /<name>.js, and add that path to PUBLIC_PATHS in server.ts.
  3. Delete the robots tag and the concept-switcher block (both are marked).

Until then the concepts live at /concepts/, behind the invite gate in production.

Claims and numbers

Prices, yields and holdings are illustrative. Companion says "many" tokenized assets trade around the clock and notes that availability depends on the asset and where you live, since many tokenized funds exclude US persons. It never promises returns or tells anyone to buy or sell.
