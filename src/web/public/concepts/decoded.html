We have examined management's assertion that, as of September 30, the fair value of the Reserve Assets held in segregated custody accounts equaled or exceeded the number of Tokens outstanding. The jargon tax

Finance has its own language.
Petyr translates.

Most people don't lose money because markets are hard. They lose it because nobody explained what the words meant.

  1. EBITDA margin compression→They're keeping less of every dollar they earn.
  2. Forward P/E of 31.4× on consensus estimates→You pay $31 for every $1 they're expected to earn next year.
  3. Trades at a 2.1% discount to NAV→The token sells for less than what's inside it.
  4. Redemptions settle T+1, subject to gating provisions→You can cash out in a day, unless the issuer pauses withdrawals.
  5. Offered to non-U.S. persons under Regulation S→Not for people in the US.
  6. Bridged representation via a third-party messaging protocol→This copy of the token was moved from another chain. The bridge can break.

Original terms

§6.1 Redemption of Tokens

Subject to the Issuer's prior completion of its onboarding procedures with respect to the redeeming Holder, Tokens may be redeemed at the then-prevailing Net Asset Value per Token, less applicable fees, provided that any redemption request shall be for not less than 50,000 Tokens. The Tokens are offered solely to persons who are not U.S. Persons. Holders who have not completed onboarding may transfer Tokens only on the secondary market, at prices which may differ materially from the Net Asset Value.
