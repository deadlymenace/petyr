The inspection

Eight checks.
Every token.

Before Petyr gives a token a verdict, it works through the same list a careful analyst would. You get the answer on the card and the detail one tap away.

Backing & issuer

What legally backs it, who issued it and where, and what you actually own.

Reserve attestation

Who checked the reserves, when, and whether they cover every token.

Redemption

Who can cash out with the issuer, how fast, and the minimums and fees.

Premium / discount

The token's price against what's inside it, and how that gap has moved.

Yield & duration

Where the yield comes from and how a move in rates would change it.

Liquidity

How much you can sell on-chain before the price starts to slip.

Chain & bridge

Native or bridged, who can mint or freeze it, and what has been audited.

Holder concentration

How much a few wallets own, and what that means when you want out.

Sources: issuer reports, on-chain data, DEX quotes, reference prices.Tokenized-asset coverage is in development.

Eligibility

Some tokens
aren't for everyone.

Many tokenized funds limit who can hold them. Petyr puts that on the card up front, so you find out before you fall for an asset.

In the US? You still get every verdict, card and brief. Where an asset excludes US persons, Petyr says so plainly.

US PERSONS EXCLUDED

Offered only outside the US, often under Regulation S.

ACCREDITED ONLY

You need to meet an income or net-worth test to buy from the issuer.

ISSUER KYC TO REDEEM

Anyone can hold it, but only verified holders can cash out with the issuer.

PERMISSIONED

Transfers only work between wallets the issuer has approved.
